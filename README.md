# [Second Place Andy's](https://m.twitch.tv/secondplaceandy/home) Ten Billy GoT Shot
The main goal is to collect a crazy huge "Hand of The King" "finishing-shot":
the "finishing" super-jackpot on the battering ram. This is done by ensuring
that your playfield-x is at 5x during each set of shots in the HOTK sequence /
lead-up and then still lit when you collect the final (huge) super-jackpot at
the battering ram at the end of the HOTK sequences.

Recall that the value for each shot in the HOTK "set" gets added together in a
"purse" that you then collect _again_ in total at the battering ram for that
final huge super collect.

So the final super-collect is also boosted by other things like the using the
flashing yellow inlanes to boost combo / shot multipliers on each lead-up shot
even further.

Suggested setup for the HOTK beast mode collect from Andy:

1. Start as house "blue", Targaryen.
   This gives you Targaryen completed for "free" and importantly, allows you to
   "freeze timers" with the action button - that's useful later, see below:
2. Race to "Hand of the King" by completing:
    * Lannister (house red)
    * Tyrell (house green)
    * Greyjoy (house purple)
3. During Hand of the King you want to keep your playfield multiplier up as
   high as possible, preferably 5x and keep it there. Use the action button if
   you are going to time out and/or hit the battering ram just once before the
   playfield-x timer runs out to keep it alive.

## Tips and Tricks
For the lead up / house battles in section 2, the most efficient way (though not
required! Just should be easier/faster):

1. Hit Tyrell shots x 2 -- green targets on RHS, both top _and_ bottom ones.
2. Hit Greyjoy shot once -- purple Orbit on the LHS.
3. Hit Tyrell middle red ramp once and you will also lock a ball due to the above
   Tyrell shots.

Repeat the shots 1. 2. 3., above three times and on the _third_ middle ramp you
will be ready for mutiball, which you should take Greyjoy and Lannister into
for battling.

Tip: Short-plunge any new ball so that it does a no-playfield-qualify and
either trap on the right flipper (hold it up or live-catch), or, better, in
some GoT instances you can dead-bounce over to the left flipper and trap there.
Now you can use the extra ball-save timer from the no-playfield-qualify to
attack the dangerous 2x Tyrell green targets during the safe ball-save
period.

Tip: Another approach to hitting the nasty Tyrell green targets is to use the
right flipper, aim at the "Lord of Light" standups on the LHS, and ricochet the
ball off them at the Tyrell green targets on the RHS. This is tricky as hell,
but has the advantage of lighting your outlane ball saver, when the
lord-of-light bank is done.

During multiball you will want to use the battering ram to collect any super
you qualify, this also will move your playfield-x multiplier along.

When you come out of multiball (or even during) if you have a playfield-x
running keep an eye on the playfield-x timer on the screen and try to keep it
alive by re-hitting the battering ram once before the timer hits zero.

Out of multiball: go battle Tyrell (and any other houses that weren't finished
during multiball) and get into HOTK, complete that with the 5x multipier
running the whole time, collect beastly final HOTK super at the battering ram.

Enjoy your ten Bs!
